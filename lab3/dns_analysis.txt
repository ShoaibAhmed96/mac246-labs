Highest Amplification Domains:
1. amp-open.example.com - 55.6x
2. txt-bulk.example.org - 35.1x

Largest Individual DNS Response:
Domain: amp-open.example.com
Qtype: ANY
Response: 3899 bytes
Amplification: 60.9x

Mitigation 1: Response Rate Limiting (RRL)
RRL reduces DNS amplification risk by limiting
excessive DNS responses from a server.

Mitigation 2: BCP 38 Ingress Filtering
BCP 38 helps prevent source IP spoofing by
filtering packets with invalid source addresses.

Conclusion:
The DNS log shows unusually large responses for
small queries, creating amplification potential.
Rate limiting and source address validation can
reduce the risk of DNS reflection attacks.
